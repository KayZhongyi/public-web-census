# Department recipes

Use these recipes to define the business question and handoff. They share the same evidence ledger but do not share unsupported conclusions. Only `competitor_intelligence` and `customer_voice` currently have deterministic analysis validators.

## Market and regional teams

**Question:** Which channels, content themes, public questions, and visible engagement patterns should inform market-entry or content hypotheses?

**Scope:** verified company-owned and relevant comparison accounts in one declared market and period.

**Deliver:** platform census, content-performance evidence, customer-question frequencies, and a short list of tests. Treat reach and engagement as signals, not sales attribution.

**Refresh:** monthly for owned channels, quarterly for stable market baselines, and before a launch or market review.

## Sales and presales

**Question:** Which publicly observable customer concerns, proof gaps, and use cases should be addressed in a proposal, presentation, or flyer?

**Scope:** selected market, partner, competitor, product, and customer-role evidence. Keep the source population explicit.

**Deliver:** evidence IDs and links grouped by buyer question, objection, use case, and missing proof. Pass reviewed findings into the presales roundtable; do not present a simulated role as interviewed customer research.

**Refresh:** before a major proposal, partner meeting, new-market launch, or material product change.

## Customer service

**Question:** What public questions, complaints, requests, and visible response patterns recur outside existing support channels?

**Scope:** declared owned accounts, selected high-value conversations, public reviews, or approved search queries.

**Deliver:** issue frequency with denominators, representative evidence, visible response coverage, and review candidates. Use the validated `customer_voice` workflow and redact public identifiers before broad sharing.

**Refresh:** monthly or after a release; high-severity findings always require human review.

## Product

**Question:** Which publicly visible usage obstacles, information gaps, or repeated requests deserve validation through product and support data?

**Scope:** product-specific public conversations with parent content and market context.

**Deliver:** observed issue clusters, frequency, severity, confidence, representative evidence, and explicit follow-up questions. Public signals supplement tickets, interviews, telemetry, and research; they do not replace them.

**Refresh:** after releases and before roadmap or documentation reviews.

## Brand and communications

**Question:** What changed in owned-channel content or public response that merits editorial or reputation review?

**Scope:** fixed owned-account watchlist and declared material-change thresholds.

**Deliver:** evidence-linked change brief distinguishing new content, material wording changes, engagement drift, and customer signals. Never alert on `not_observed` as deletion.

**Refresh:** weekly or monthly according to channel activity; avoid unattended open-ended surveillance.

## Handoff minimum

Every cross-department handoff must include:

- target, market, source boundary, cutoff, and collection status;
- observation separated from interpretation and recommendation;
- counts with denominators and stable evidence IDs or source URLs;
- known gaps, identity uncertainty, and sample-size limits;
- owner, requested decision, review status, and next refresh date.
